```

  ├─ Alert indicators

Amber Yellow:    #FFC107
  ├─ Disclaimer boxes

  └─ Caution indicators

  ├─ Medical-feel
  └─ Professional backdrop

```

  • 12px border radius
  • Plain white background

AFTER:
  • 6px blue left border + 3px blue top border
  • Multiple enhanced shadows
  • 16px border radius (modern, friendly)
  • Gradient backgrounds on some cards
  • Smooth hover effects (lifts up)
  • Professional depth and dimension
